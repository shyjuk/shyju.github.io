---
title: "Hide Ubuntu 18 top panel"
date: 2018-05-10
categories: 
  - "technical"
---

Login to your ubuntu 18 system. Open Ubuntu software(Press Windows Key) type in "Hide Top Bar", you can find Hide Top Bar GNOME Shell Extension. Install it , open the application and hide the top panel. You have lot of options like autohide ,slide animation etc.

How to import Nokia Contacts to Gmail

Copy all contact .vcf  to Computer .

take command prompt and change to the folder where you copied nokia vcf files.

Run the command  copy /B \*.vcf contacts.vcf

Open the contacts.vcf with notepad and Do a find and replace with following words.

find: N:

Replace:  FN:

 

Save and close the file.

Goto Gmail contacts and import the contact,vcf.

Thats it. Your all contacts are imported to Gmail. Take your android phone and syn your contacts
